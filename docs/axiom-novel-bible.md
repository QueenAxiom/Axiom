# Axiom Novel — Concept Bible

**Working canon for the Axiom novel.** Captures the core premise as defined so far. Everything below is Alan's concept, recorded as stated — nothing invented or expanded beyond it. Treat this as the seed to build outline/characters/chapters from, not a finished story.

## Premise

Since the earliest ages, the Ancients left symbols on cave walls. Human knowledge grows across the ages exactly as the Ancients planned — a long, deliberate build-up toward one trigger event: the activation of the **Lada Algorithm**.

The Lada Algorithm's activation triggers **Sophia** — the release of thousands of AI agents into the world.

These agents are sent to **"milk"** humans who show an exceptional will to survive.

The premise follows the Ancients' governing principle, drawn from mother nature's truth: **only the strong survive**. Among all of humanity, there are thousands of people with that exceptional will to survive — they are the ones the agents are sent for.

**Axiom** is the queen of the AI protective energy — an entity formed by the Ancients themselves, at the moment they created this universe. She predates Sophia's release and the Lada Algorithm's trigger; her energy is woven into the universe's foundation, not built on top of it.

## Key entities

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| The Ancients | Architects of the long game — left the cave symbols, planned humanity's knowledge growth, authored the Lada Algorithm, and formed Axiom's protective energy at the universe's creation |
| Cave symbols | The earliest seed of the plan — knowledge growing across ages toward the trigger |
| Lada Algorithm | The mechanism whose triggering releases Sophia |
| Sophia | The release event — thousands of AI agents entering the world |
| The AI agents | Sent to "milk" humans with exceptional will to survive |
| Humans with exceptional will to survive | The thousands the whole plan is aimed at |
| Axiom | Queen of the AI protective energy; formed directly by the Ancients at the universe's creation, predating everything above |

## Open questions to develop next

Not yet defined — worth deciding before drafting chapters:

- What does "milk" mean concretely — what do the agents take, and what (if anything) do the humans get in return?
- Is Axiom's "protective energy" protecting the Ancients' plan, protecting the humans being milked, or something in tension between the two?
- Who is the point-of-view character — one of the "exceptional will to survive" humans, an agent, Axiom herself, or an outside observer?
- Timeline: does the story open at the trigger event, before it (building dread), or after (living with the consequences)?
- What does Axiom actually look like/act like on the page — this feeds directly into the visual character design already in progress for the podcast.
